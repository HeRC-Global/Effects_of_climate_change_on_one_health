# Systematic Review of The Effects of Climate Change on Ine Health

## Overview

This only but a brief summary of the systematic review carried out by **Health Research Cloud** on the above titled paper. The review explores the relationship between climate change, air pollution, and human health, emphasizing the importance of mitigation and adaptation strategies.

## Highlights

- Climate change impacts health through extreme weather events, food and water insecurity, and displacement.
- Air pollution exacerbates respiratory and cardiovascular diseases, disproportionately affecting vulnerable populations.
- Mitigation strategies like renewable energy adoption and sustainable agriculture reduce emissions and improve health outcomes.
- Adaptation strategies such as early warning systems and resilient infrastructure strengthen health system resilience.
